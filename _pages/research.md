## Research Vision

In engineering systems, degradation, performance, and operational decisions evolve together: degradation limits what a system can do, and operating decisions in turn shape how it degrades. My long-term vision is to enable systems that anticipate how degradation changes what they can reliably accomplish and adapt operations in response. Toward this vision, I pursue three directions: **1) Learning system capability** from heterogeneous data, including sensor signals, events, and operator logs. **2) Predicting capability evolution** under different decisions with digital twins and uncertainty quantification. **3) Optimizing operations** as capability evolves, with reinforcement learning and mathematical programming. I study these across physical scales, from material and component degradation to asset- and system-level performance, so that decisions at every scale inform how degradation and capability evolve. I apply these ideas in smart manufacturing, logistics robotics, and fusion and nuclear energy systems.
